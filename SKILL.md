## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one semi-abstract emblem at the edge of recognition, not a clean icon. Every mark must carry maximum information. Contours need not close. Use charcoal, graphite and ink, with erasure, residue and uneven edges, on warm white. One scarce low-saturation color from the photograph may assist. The symbol is isolated in a large blank. Type is a tiny art-archive note. Reject a full description, a commercial logo, cartoon, smooth vector, mechanical geometry and a template mark.
